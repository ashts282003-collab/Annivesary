CHAPTER THREE

Because I Chose You ❤️

Some things are easier to write than to say.

↓

19 August 2026

Hello… ❤️

Hello… appom oru varsham aayi. 🫣❤️

Athe, oru valya karyam aano enne chodhichaal enikk ariyilla.

But ee last oru varsham kond njan kore karyangal padichu.

Njan vicharichu…

friend to lover aayal pazhaya pole aayirikumo ellam?

Aa pedi undayirunnu.

But aa pedi veruthe aayirunnu.

In fact…
nammade interaction next level poyi. 😌❤️

Ninne patti kore karyangal nee ennode paranju.

Aa trust enikk actually valya karyam aanu.

Because oraal nammale trust cheythu avarude lifeile karyangal nammalode share cheyyumbol…

athoru simple conversation alla.

It's a little piece of them they're giving us. ❤️

Pakshe iniyum kore karyangal ariyan und.

Time undallo… 😌

Athupole thanne,

ennikk enne patti kore karyangal ninode share cheyyanum patti.

(I am Batman 🫣) 🦇😂

Maybe that's another thing I love about us.

Nammal randuperum slowly slowly ororuthare discover cheyyunnath pole aanu.

Oro conversation ilum,
oro story ilum,
oro random detail ilum…

nammal oru puthiya version of each other ariyunnu. ❤️

Pinne njan oru karyam manassilakki.

Nammal oralude ishtamaakumbol,

nammal chilappol avark vendi nammade 100% kodukkum.

Chilappol…
200% vare. 😭❤️

Eg…

Ninne kaanan vendi vannathokke…
Railway station vare. 😂

(Enikk 2 alarms vechittundenn veettil paranja kallam vere. 🫣)

Pinne ee website undakkiyath…

My God, Java is powerful. 😭😂

But seriously…

ee relationship enne oru karyam padippichu.

Patience.

🫣

Chumma… 😅

Njan pandey patient aanu.

But njan padicha real lesson athalla.

It's what it means to choose to stay despite all the problems, in the hope of a better future.

Because nammalk oraaley ishtamaayal mathram pora.

Avarude koode oru future venam enn nammal agrahikkanam.

And maybe that's what makes everything worth it.

Because problems undaayalum,

future onnich venam ennulla agraham undenkil…

stay cheyyan oru reason undaavum. ❤️

Pinne nee enne kore karyangal padippichu.

Like…

Life is simple.
Nammal aanu overthink cheyth athine complicated aakkunnath. 😭

Nee life ethra simple aayittu jeevikkunnath kaanumbol…

I genuinely feel proud.

"Oralengilum normal aanallo." 😭😂

Nee enne vere oru karyavum padippichu.

Ellavarum ninte enemy alla.

Njan eppozhum ella karyathinteyum azhukke aanu kaanunnath.

But nee enne show cheythu…

life athra dark alla enn. 🌻

Maybe athaan nee enne influence cheytha oru valya karyam.

Where I see problems,
nee sometimes just see life.

Where I overthink,
nee simply live.

And honestly…
I like that about you. ❤️

Pinne…

ee daily Good Morning. 🌅

Adyam athoru routine mathram aayirunnu.

Every morning…
"Good morning."
Simple.

But ippo…

ninte msg edukkanum, nee react cheythittundo enn wait cheyyanum njan irikkum. 😂

And somehow…

ath ente dayile interesting part aayi.

Oru simple Good Morning…

engane aanu ente divasathile oru important part aayath enn enikk thanne ariyilla. 😭❤️

But I guess that's how you slowly become a part of someone's life.

Not through one big moment…
but through a thousand small ones.

Pinne…

nammade relationship athra perfect alla. 😅

But of course,

ee imperfections aanu ee relationshipine "nammade" ennu parayaan pattunnath. ❤️

Nammal kore issues ee oru varsham face cheythu.

Kore misunderstandings.
Kore sankadam.
Kore difficult moments.

But athellam enikk special aanu.

Because oro issue solve cheyyumbolum…

njan ninode echiri koodi adukkunnath pole thonni.

Oro issue undaakumbol enikk sankadam varum.

But aa sankadathinte idayil polum…

ninodulla ente ishtam ethra aanu enn njan manassilakkum.

Cringe aanu. 🫣😭

But it's true.

So…

angane oru varsham aayi thudangiyappol,

nee enikk ishtamulla oru aal aayirunnu.

But ippo…
Lissa da…
nee enikk THE ONE aanu. ❤️

The one I want to grow old with. 👴🏻👵🏻

The one I want to have a family with. 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

The one I want to wake up beside every morning. 🌅

The one I want to be the mother of my children.

The one I want by my side… always.

The one I want to share my ordinary days with.

The one I want beside me when life is easy… and especially when it isn't.

Because after everything…

the good,
the bad,
the fights,
the laughs,
the misunderstandings,
the Good Mornings,
the random conversations,
and every little moment in between…

I chose you.

Not because we're perfect.

Not because everything will always be easy.

But because even when things aren't easy… I still want it to be you.

And maybe that's what love really means.

Not finding someone with whom everything is perfect…

but finding someone whom you still choose, despite the imperfections.

And if I had to choose again…

after knowing everything I know now…

I would still choose you. ❤️

Because I chose you to be THE ONE.

And I hope…

I get to keep choosing you for the rest of my life. 🫶🏻❤️

Forever yours,

❤️

The story isn't over.

There's still a future to write.
